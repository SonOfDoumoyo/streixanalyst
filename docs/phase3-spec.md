# StreixAnalyst: Phase 3 spec (not built yet)

Status: stored for later. Do not build until the owner says so.

## Submissions and grading (classroom)
- Several kinds of work count as a submission (practice attempts, strategies, analyses, and similar).
- Teachers can grade and comment on submissions.
- The model learns from those grades and comments too.
- Students join a class with both a class code and their email.

## Classroom as an organization feature
- Classroom is a feature for people on the Organization plan ($30/month).
- Organization members are "team" or "organization" members. Do not label anyone "teacher" in the UI.
- Whoever adds teammates or students to the organization is its creator (may also be called teacher or guru).
- The creator adds members by their email and can delete them.
- Members can leave the organization.
- Students added to an organization get full organization access, with no student limits.
- Organization has more features than Pro. Pro must still get everything it paid for.

## Plans and trial
- Free plan: limited features.
- Pro plan: $15/month.
- Organization plan: $30/month.
- Trial: 30 days from release, for all users. It is a general launch trial, not 30 days free for every new user. Show a countdown timer.

## Look and feel
- Modern, clean, very polished, similar to TradingView, including fonts.
- Dark mode: black background with a white logo.
- Light mode: black logo.

## Speed and responsiveness
- The site must feel fast and be responsive on phones, tablets and desktop.
- This is still version 1. Keep the scope to what is listed here.

## Live chart
- The chart must be live, showing real market data and real orders, not a fake or simulated chart.

## Payments
- The web app must verify and confirm each payment itself. The owner will not be available to check payments by hand.
